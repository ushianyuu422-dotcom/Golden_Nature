About the Resort

A Nature-Lover’s Haven

As soon as you arrive, you’ll notice the difference: the air is cooler, the sound of gently flowing water replaces traffic noise, and the vantage of the pool beneath a canopy of trees invites you to unwind. The resort celebrates its natural surroundings rather than overshadowing them, giving guests space to relax, dip into clear water, and enjoy the slow rhythms of nature.

Escape to a refreshing paradise hidden in the heart of Tupi, South Cotabato. Golden Nature Cold Spring Resort offers the perfect place to relax, unwind, and reconnect with nature. Surrounded by lush greenery and powered by a natural cold spring, this peaceful getaway is ideal for families, friends, and anyone seeking a calm and scenic escape.
